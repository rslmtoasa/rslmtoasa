## Convergence behavior and source audit

The repeated slow residuals do not establish a numerical floor. The outer mixer computes `mix%delta` as the normalized RMS difference of packed `qia_old` and `qia_new` values (`source/mix.f90:693-696`); the default outer threshold is `0.5d-8` (`source/self.f90:3597-3599`), and `is_converged` also requires the fixed-U residual streak when active (`source/self.f90:4764-4770`). Separately, the radial self-consistency uses a hard-coded 80-iteration cap and internal tolerances `tol=1e-6`, `tolrsq=1e-8` (`source/self.f90:4976-4985`). These distinct metrics/tolerances are plausible contributors to stalled outer updates, but neither source nor current logs prove causation. Finite energy-grid integration is another possible influence, but this campaign has no refinement data to test it. Conclusion: the cause is undetermined; the registered criteria and thresholds remain unchanged. The bounded continuations/trial recorded above are the only further attempts; a failed cap leaves the corresponding comparison incomplete at RA STOP.
