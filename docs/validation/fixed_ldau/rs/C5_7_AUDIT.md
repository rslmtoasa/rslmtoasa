# C5.7 final audit

**Verdict: T1 passes; T2a fails at runtime; KS acceptance is unevaluated. Adoption and merge are not permitted.** This audit checked the committed guard source, all T1 fixture logs and identity records, and both invocations in the archived T2a raw packet. It launched no runs.

The fixed executable is SHA-256 `07a94dd35208ed7ace3b3c729f7d122e5771924265035a57606f7b84a446ea10`; `source/self.f90` hashes to `33c33aa6d6a49a25dc0015e45a9a30143b83f5e9ebb5b4510485df1dab0240ba`. The committed guard derives recursion ownership from `irec` cluster sites mapped through `iz` to symbolic types, and frozen-host ownership from `ib` sites mapped through `iz`; it does not infer ownership from ordinal position. Strict mode consistency and the legacy unknown-mode WARNING apply to frozen U-carrying hosts. Updated atoms may rewrite mode/backend provenance, and impurity auto selection examines frozen hosts.

All 13 RA2 fixture logs exist and match their recorded SHA-256 values. The required bulk legacy and spectral-seed conversions log INFO and write current provenance. The frozen local-host/forced-spectral fixture and surface frozen-host mismatch fixture terminate on the fatal mismatch. The legacy frozen-host fixture logs that its unknown mode is treated as spectral and requests a host restart; the run selects spectral. The added surface recursion ownership fixture logs seed conversion and rewrite. The exact single-thread no-U identity gate passes: 27 RS and 24 KS artifacts match under the registered exclusions and `str.out` normalization, with no mismatches.

Both T2a invocations passed preflight, used `use_kspace=true`, the fixed binary, the same accepted legacy seed and full single-thread caps, and resolved auto to local. The archived logs independently show LDM completion and `mix.f90` charge-transfer output followed by SIGSEGV (exit 139) and address-only backtraces. Each trace has 1,219,081 partial rows for iteration 1, zero `POST_SCF_RADIAL` rows, and no paired state capture. Therefore no full outer iteration or 20-iteration acceptance window exists. The crash cause remains unexplained; this audit makes no claim that it is pre-existing or newly introduced.

Both run `Fe_scf.nml` and `Fe_out.nml` remain byte-identical to the protected accepted checkpoint, SHA-256 `bb647254a5612f53d00e2745026c2cde32511f4363a1c7c3a1331d06573ead6b`; no new local/KS provenance was written. The periodic free-space minimum was 13,805,854,720 bytes, above the 1 GiB floor. Lifecycle records show both solver exits and the packet reports owned PIDs confirmed exited.

**Protocol deviation:** attempt 2 was a fresh same-seed invocation after attempt 1 left no complete state. It was not a registered continuation and had already launched before the corrective stop instruction arrived. The packet records two of three attempts used; no third attempt was launched. Following the T2a failure stop, T2b, T3 and T4 were not run. No density adoption or merge is allowed by this evidence.
